Screen mirror.

See exactly what your key's screen is showing, live on your computer, over the USB cable. Handy before the screen is wired in, for reading the pairing code, and for screenshots.

  1. Flash the key with firmware 0.2.1 or newer, the version with the mirror.
  2. Plug the key into this computer with a USB-C cable.
  3. Click Connect and pick the port that says XIAO or Seeed. On a Mac it may show as usbmodem.
  4. Close the Arduino Serial Monitor first. Only one program can use the port at a time.
