L-02 COOKIES

Cookie policy.

UPDATED NOVEMBER 18, 2026

This site sets no cookies. There's no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What's saved in your browser

  • nodCustom: the custom requests you make in the Try it section, so they're still there when you come back. It stays until you clear your browser's site data.
  • nodFill: a one-time note that plays the Pre-order list animation when you click it from another page. It's deleted right after, or when you close the tab.

Neither one is sent anywhere. You can clear both in your browser's settings for this site.

Other services the page loads

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, like your IP address.
  • The fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for them.
  • The 3D key on the home page uses a code library loaded from jsDelivr.
  • When you send a form, what you typed goes to Formspree, which emails it to me.

Each of these sees a normal web request from your browser and has its own privacy policy. None of them sets cookies through this site.

If that ever changes, for example when payments are added, this page changes first.
