Nod for individuals.

A 1 x 1 x 3 inch key with a screen. When your AI tool needs a yes, it asks here instead of waiting in a tab you forgot about.

A-01 HOW IT WORKS
TAP

Approve

It only counts once the card has landed, so a stray press can't answer something you haven't read.

DOUBLE-TAP

Deny

The tool gets a plain no.

HOLD 1.0 S

Approve something risky

Deletes and force pushes ignore taps. You have to mean it.

Try it on the home page, it works with your mouse or space bar.

A-02 MAKE YOUR OWN

Send it anything.

Any script can ask Nod first: a deploy, a purchase, a cron job, a message to your team. Pick a name and a color, try it on the key, then copy the two lines below.

  A-03 WORKS WITH
  

    
Works with what you already use

    

      
Claude CodeAsks Nod before it runs a command. If the key is off, you get the normal prompt.

      
git pushShows the branch and how many commits. Force pushes need the hold.

      
Codex, Gemini CLI and othersAnything that can ask before it acts can ask Nod, with one command: nod ask.

      
Claude desktop appTalks to Nod over Bluetooth through the app's developer mode. Anthropic says that feature isn't officially supported.

    

    
Nothing is ever approved by default. No key, and you get asked the usual way.
