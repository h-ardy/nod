Nod server.

The key is how a person says yes. The Nod server is what makes that yes count, for any request you send it, on any machine your team uses.

I'm building the server now. The key, the laptop app and site checks work today. Everything else on this page is marked with when it's coming.

S-01 HOW IT WORKS

A yes that happens off the laptop.

Today the key talks to the app on your own computer. That's fine for one person. The server sits between your AI tools and the things that matter: deploys, your database, payments. It holds the passwords for those, and it only uses them after a key signs for the exact action on its screen.

S-02 CUSTOM PUSHES

Anything can ask.

Not just AI tools. A deploy script, a refund over $100, a cron job that deletes old data, a message going out to every customer. You decide who gets the request, how many keys it needs, and the name and color it shows on the screen.

S-03 SECURITY

Built so a yes can't be faked.

An AI tool can be tricked by a bad instruction hidden in a web page or a file. Malware can pretend to be you. Neither can press a key on your desk, so the server only trusts presses it can check.

Signed pressesEach press is signed by a secret that never leaves the key. Nothing on the laptop can make one.Next
Passwords stay on the serverThe laptop and the AI tool never hold the deploy or database passwords, so they can't skip the key.Next
What you see is what you signThe key signs what its own screen shows. If someone swaps the action after you read it, the signature won't match.Next
A record of every yesWhat ran, who approved it, when, and the signature. Ready when an auditor asks.Later

What it stops

An AI tool running something risky without a person pressing a key. Malware on a laptop faking a press. Someone changing the action after you read it.

What it doesn't

Anything that never goes through Nod, like a password still saved on a laptop. Someone holding your key. That's why the passwords that matter move to the server.

S-04 VIBECODE PROTECTION

Built your site with AI? Protect it.

Sites written mostly by AI tools tend to ship with the same holes: keys left in the code, pages anyone can open, forms that trust anything. Fixing them once isn't enough when the next prompt can bring them back.

A check before launchI test your site the way an attacker would, show you what got through, and help you fix it.Now
A press before productionChanges to your live site, your database or your payments wait for your key, so the AI tool can't ship them alone.Next
